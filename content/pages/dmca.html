Report Infringing or Mistagged Content

Online report submission is unavailable. Email your copyright notice to hello@lbry.com.

Include the following information:

  • Your name and contact information, including your email address.
  • The copyright holder's name and your authority to act on their behalf.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work and the infringing content, including its LBRY URL or claim identifier.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the copyright holder, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement that the information is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act for the copyright holder.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

See the DMCA article for reporting requirements. No report has been submitted through this page.

You may also submit content that you want to flag for being illegal, not marked Mature, or abusing the tag system. To learn more about reporting infringing or illegal content and DMCA procedures, please see our DMCA article.
